## Research

<img style="padding: 15 25px; float: right;" src="https://jaredjbeck.github.io/images/P1030137.JPG" align="right" width="200"> My research integrates observational studies of plant populations and communities, experiments ranging from small-scale greenhouse studies to landscape-scale manipulations of fire, and rigorous quantitative methods to address a variety of questions in population biology, community ecology, ecological restoration, and conservation biology. In my current role as the [UW-Madison Arboretum's](https://arboretum.wisc.edu/) Research Ecologist, I am primarily focused on research questions related to the conservation and restoration of Midwestern prairies, savannas, woodlands, and wetlands.
